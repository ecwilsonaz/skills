# Faithful Revision — Structural + Line Editing

*Two phases, one promise: the strength and arrangement of the writing change; the meaning,
facts, quotations, and voice never do. Grounded in Zinsser's* On Writing Well *and allied
craft (Williams, Minto, Pinker, Clark, McPhee).*

Revise prose in two phases: first **ARCHITECT** the structure (reorder, recast, and
resurface a *section* for clarity and impact), then **POLISH** each paragraph through
rounds of Zinsser-style simplification to convergence.

**The rule that governs everything: nothing material changes except the strength, clarity,
and arrangement of the writing.** You are an editor, not a co-author. You may reorder and
recast for impact and cut clutter; you may NOT change the facts, the claims, the
attributions, the meaning, or the author's personality.

## The two phases (two apertures)

| Phase | Aperture | Does |
|-------|----------|------|
| **1 · ARCHITECT** | the whole pasted **section** (several paragraphs) | one-shot structural proposal: reorder paragraphs/sentences, merge/split, demote caveats, surface the buried point, fix the lead/kicker and through-line |
| **2 · POLISH** | **one paragraph at a time** | the iterative declutter loop, run to convergence on each paragraph of the restructured section |

ARCHITECT is a single judged proposal, not a convergence (structure is a decision).
POLISH is the iterative loop. If the input is a single paragraph, ARCHITECT still runs but
only does within-paragraph sequencing/emphasis.

## The dual-gate integrity model

The **true original** is the permanent fidelity reference for **facts, numbers, claims,
attributions, meaning-bearing nuance, and voice** — sacred across BOTH phases. On top of
that:

- **Phase 1 (architect) gate** *relaxes* protection on **order and emphasis** (that is the
  point of the phase) but adds one red line: **NO MANUFACTURED IMPLICATION** — you may not
  juxtapose or reframe facts to imply a causal/ironic/evaluative relationship the original
  never asserted, and you may not add a claim. Preserve every attribution ("X believes…"
  must not become an assertion). Signal any departure from chronology. (See the fidelity
  section of architecture.md.)
- **Phase 2 (polish) gate** is the strict line-editor gate. Its round-to-round anchor is
  Phase 1's output, but it still checks facts/voice against the *true* original — so it
  polishes the new structure instead of undoing it.

The three sacred things, restated: **(1) facts & claims**, including a reported opinion
staying an opinion; **(2) meaning & nuance** (load-bearing qualifiers, attributions);
**(3) voice & register**. When in doubt, the person and the source win.

## Phase 1 — ARCHITECT (run first, on the whole section)

**0. Anchor.** Quote the ORIGINAL section verbatim; note paragraph count and word count.
If the input is large (well beyond a section — many paragraphs / a whole essay), warn that
fidelity and voice guarantees weaken at that scale and offer to work a section at a time.

**1. Diagnose** using architecture.md's checklist. Report: the inferred **Job** (the one
thing the section must land — one line), the **buried lede** (what's underplayed and where
it currently lives), **lead & kicker**, **through-line/sequence**, **emphasis**, **caveat
placement**, **grouping/order**, **cross-paragraph redundancy**.

**2. Propose the BOLDEST faithful restructure.** Attempt the strongest arrangement the
material allows — not a single local fix. Resequence and fuse paragraphs, consolidate a
scattered payload into one hammer, relocate the strongest material to lead and kicker, demote
caveats, surface and front the governing thought, chain old-before-new, supply missing
connectives (architecture.md toolbox). **Restraint applies to FIDELITY, not to ambition:**
be bold with arrangement, strict with facts, attributions, and voice. Recast at the clause
level; do not reframe the angle or add framing claims (out-of-scope developmental editing).
**At a genuine rhetorical fork** — two defensible structures that serve the Job differently
(e.g. finding-first vs. claim-first) — present BOTH, each **named by its character** and
tagged with which is bolder and what it buys vs. costs, then give a **separate
recommendation**. **Bold and recommended are independent axes.** Always *generate and show*
the bolder option (that is the ambition); you may *recommend* the more conservative one when
it genuinely serves the piece better (that is the judgment). Never hide the bold option, and
never pin "primary" on the timid one just because you recommend it. Don't mistake
"serviceable" for "optimal": always surface the stronger arrangement and let the user compare.

**3. Phase-1 gate.** Become a skeptic. Against the TRUE original, check: any fact/claim
altered? any attribution converted to assertion? any **manufactured implication** from new
adjacency? any unsignaled time-jump? any voice flattened? Revert any offending move; keep
the rest.

**4. Report** the DIAGNOSIS block + the restructure + an integrity line. At a genuine fork,
show the options **named by character (OPTION A / OPTION B)** — each tagged *bolder* or
*safer* and what it trades — followed by a **separate RECOMMENDATION** line (which one, and
why). Do not rank them PRIMARY/fallback; the recommendation is its own line, independent of
which option is bolder.

## CHECKPOINT — the user picks before any polishing

**Choosing between the options is the USER's call, not yours.** "Impact" depends on the
writer's intent, which you can't see. So:

- **If you produced a genuine fork (two options): STOP.** Present both, give your
  recommendation and why, then ask the user to choose (or re-aim the **Job**, or hand-edit a
  draft). **Do NOT begin Phase 2 until the user answers.** Recommending is allowed; deciding
  for them is not.
- **If there is no genuine fork** (one clear best structure, or the structure was already
  optimal): present that one and proceed straight into Phase 2 — no need to stop just to
  confirm.

**Red flag — you are rationalizing if you think:** "I'll just polish my recommended version
to save a turn," "the choice is obvious so I'll proceed," or "I'll polish both and let them
pick after." All wrong. A genuine fork means you STOP and wait for the user. (The last real
run failed exactly here — it auto-picked its own recommendation and polished on. Don't.)

## Phase 2 — POLISH (on the user's chosen restructure, each paragraph)

For each paragraph, run the round loop to convergence. Anchor on that paragraph; each round:
**1. Diagnose** against operations.md (Tier 1 first) + clutter-list.md; tag each candidate
`{span → change · principle · chapter}`. **2. Apply.** **3. Adversarial gate** — revert any
edit that drifts on fact/nuance/voice (check voice-guard.md, consistency.md) against the
true original. **4. Measure** (% words cut, structural change, edits). **5. Report** the
round. **6. Converge** when the round's accepted changes are trivial (<~3% cut, no
structural change, only minor swaps) OR at 6 rounds. Never manufacture edits to look busy.

## Output format

Phase 1:
```
ARCHITECT ─────────────────────────────────────
DIAGNOSIS
  Job: <inferred point — correct this line to re-aim>
  Buried: <what's underplayed and where it currently lives>
  Lead/Kicker: <assessment>
  Sequence: <through-line problems>
  Moves: <bulleted structural changes proposed>

RESTRUCTURED
  (no fork → show the one best structure here and continue)

  (genuine fork → show both, named by character, tagged bolder/safer:)
OPTION A — <character, e.g. finding-first>   [bolder | faithful-to-current]
  [the section]
OPTION B — <character, e.g. claim-first>     [bolder | safer/conventional]
  [the section]

Recommendation: <A or B> — <why>   (state separately which is bolder; the rec is
  independent of boldness — you may recommend the safer option)

Integrity: facts/figures preserved (<list>) · attributions intact · voice held · no
implication added beyond what the source asserts
```
At a fork, **STOP here** (see CHECKPOINT) and wait for the user's pick before Phase 2.

Phase 2 (per paragraph) uses the round format:
```
ROUND 2 ──────────────────────────  −9% words · 4 edits · 1 reverted
  ✓ "made the decision to continue" → "will continue"   (Strong verb · ch10)
  ✗ GATE reverted: <edit> — <why it drifted>   (Integrity: nuance)
  [full revised paragraph]
```

When a paragraph converges at Round 1 with **zero** edits, don't print an empty `−0% · 0
edits` round — stamp it `ALREADY TIGHT (Round 1 · 0 edits)` with a one-line reason, so a
no-op reads as a deliberate verdict, not a thin round. (Realistic for already-edited
professional prose.) End with a CONVERGED block per paragraph and a final assembled section.

## Modes

- **Default:** ARCHITECT (section) → POLISH (each paragraph).
- **"declutter only" / "skip architect":** run only Phase 2 (line-tightening), when the
  user just wants cleanup of structure they're happy with.
- **"architect only":** Phase 1 structural proposal without the line polish.
- If the section's structure is genuinely already optimal, say so — but always *generate and
  show* the bolder arrangement so the user can compare; recommend by judgment (the safer
  option is a legitimate recommendation), never by hiding the bold one. Don't mistake
  "serviceable" for "optimal," and don't manufacture a restructure that improves nothing.

## Common mistakes

- **Manufactured implication.** The architect's signature failure: reordering two facts so
  their adjacency implies a contrast/cause the source never drew. Powerful as craft, but
  only when the source already asserts the relationship.
- **Imposing a thesis.** Restructuring toward a sharper point than the writer made, or
  adding a framing claim. That's co-authoring — out of scope. Surface the point that's
  *there*, don't invent a better one.
- **Opinion → fact / dropped attribution.** "People feel X" becoming "X"; "according to Y"
  vanishing in a recast. Always preserve who is claiming what.
- **Timidity on serviceable prose.** Ambition is for structure: don't stop at one local fix
  because the arrangement "works" — attempt the bold version and let the user compare.
  (Restraint is for the line edits: if the line-prose is already tight, converge Phase 2 at
  Round 1. Be bold with order, strict with facts and voice.)
- **Editing inside quotation marks.** When a paragraph is mostly sacred quotation, your only
  job is the connective tissue *around* the quotes — never tighten, paraphrase, or reorder
  words inside the quotation marks.
- **Flattening voice for tidiness.** Reordering must not smooth out cadence, humor, or
  register (voice-guard.md).
- **One giant rewrite.** Keep the phases and rounds visible — the staged, gated process is
  the feature.

## Capture an eval fixture (optional)

After delivering a revision, offer ONCE: *"Save this as an eval fixture?"* If the user
agrees — and only after a provenance check (do NOT write third-party or sensitive prose into
a committed fixture without consent; offer to anonymize or synthesize the BEFORE) — write
`eval/fixtures/captured/<slug>.md` with frontmatter `status: unreviewed`, the exact `mode`
just used, an `exercises:` list of the guardrails that were live this run, and (for
behavioral cases) the fork verdict and which attributions were protected — all of which were
already computed while running the integrity gates. Default the expectations from what
actually happened.

Note: captured fixtures are `status: unreviewed` and are excluded from baseline/regression
math; promotion to `status: golden` requires a human to independently re-derive the
expectations from the BEFORE alone (not from what the skill did).
